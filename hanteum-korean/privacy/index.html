Privacy policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026
Developer: Minor Orbit (Play developer display name: bibiTools)
Android package: com.minororbit.hanteum.campus
Contact: davidleepub@gmail.com

This policy applies to Hanteum Korean: Campus Life, including the initial pilot and versions with voice practice. The app has no account system, advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK, or developer-operated learning-data server.

1. Learning information on your device

Bookmarks, lesson progress, practice history, guide checkmarks, and settings are stored in private app storage on your device. The developer does not receive your learning records through the app.

The app disables automatic Android backup and excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. You can remove local learning records using Android’s Clear storage setting or by uninstalling the app.

2. Message drafts and clipboard

Message templates use your entries to create a temporary preview in memory. The app clears the draft when you leave or recreate the form. It does not save the entries to its learning records or send them to the developer.

When you choose Copy, Android places the preview on its clipboard. You choose where to paste it and whether to send it. Android, your keyboard, and the destination app handle the text under their own settings and policies. Avoid entering unnecessary student IDs or sensitive personal information.

3. Optional microphone access and voice practice

On versions with voice practice, the app asks for microphone access when you choose to record. It records up to 30 seconds at a time and holds the recording temporarily in its private cache so you can listen back. The app does not upload or share the recording or use it for automated scoring.

You can delete the recording in Voice practice. The app clears it when you change the practice expression, leave the practice screen, or switch apps. It clears any leftover file the next time the app opens. Learning text and example audio remain available if you decline microphone access.

4. Lesson audio and Android text-to-speech

On versions with included lesson audio, example recordings are prepared before distribution and played on your device. When an included recording is unavailable, these versions can play fixed Korean learning text using an installed offline Korean Android voice. The initial pilot uses the Android text-to-speech engine for lesson audio. The engine may process speech according to its own settings and privacy policy.

The app does not pass voice recordings or message entries to the speech engine or call a cloud speech-generation API during lessons. Your Android speech engine manages its own voice downloads and settings. You can continue reading the lessons if a voice is unavailable.

5. Websites and policy hosting

Opening the Hangul learning site, an external school or information link, or this policy uses your browser and the destination service, which can process normal web-request information under their own policies.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub can process request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Hosting this policy does not provide the developer with an app learning-data backend.

6. Deletion and security

To remove all local app records, use Android Settings → Apps → Hanteum Korean: Campus Life → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This also removes progress and settings. Copied messages, messages you send through another app, and speech-engine downloads must be managed separately in those services.

The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete records stored only on your device. The app uses Android private app storage; no additional end-to-end encryption is claimed.

7. Support and policy changes

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request and retain correspondence as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. You can request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to those obligations. Please identify Hanteum Korean: Campus Life and avoid sending passwords, student IDs, or unnecessary personal information.

We update this policy and its effective date when our handling of information changes.
