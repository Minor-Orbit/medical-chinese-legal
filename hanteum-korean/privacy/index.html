Hanteum Korean: Campus Life

Privacy policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
Developer: Minor Orbit (Play developer display name: bibiTools)
Android package: com.minororbit.hanteum.campus
Contact: davidleepub@gmail.com

This policy applies only to the Hanteum Korean: Campus Life 0.1.0 pilot. It does not describe the other apps hosted on this website. The pilot has no account system, advertising SDK, developer analytics SDK, or developer-operated learning-data server.

1. Learning information on your device

Saved expression identifiers, lesson progress, and learning settings are kept in the app’s private storage on your device. The developer does not receive your bookmarks, quiz answers, or learning history through the app. The pilot does not request Internet, microphone, location, or contacts permissions.

The app disables automatic Android backup and excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Local learning records remain until you remove them through the available app controls, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

2. Message drafts and clipboard

The meeting-request form uses the details you enter to create a preview. Those personal entries are not written to the app’s persistent learning storage or sent to the developer. Leaving or recreating the form may clear the draft.

Only when you choose Copy is the preview placed on the Android clipboard. Android and any app you paste the message into handle that text under their own policies. The app does not send emails or messages for you. Avoid entering unnecessary student IDs or sensitive personal information.

3. Optional device text-to-speech

When you choose to listen, fixed Korean learning text is passed to the Android text-to-speech engine selected on your device. The app does not record your voice and does not pass your meeting-request entries to the speech engine.

Your speech engine may download voices, use network processing, or process diagnostics according to its own settings and provider’s privacy policy. Check your Android text-to-speech settings for the engine in use. You can continue learning with text if a Korean voice is unavailable or you do not use speech.

This pilot does not call Google Cloud TTS, fal.ai, or ElevenLabs generation APIs and does not include prerecorded MP3 audio.

4. Websites and policy hosting

Opening an external school or information link uses your browser and the destination service, which can process normal web-request information under their own policies.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub can process request information, including IP addresses, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Hosting this policy does not provide the developer with an app learning-data backend.

5. Deletion and security

To remove all local app records, use Android Settings → Apps → Hanteum Korean: Campus Life → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This also removes progress and settings. Copied messages, messages you send through another app, and speech-engine downloads must be managed separately in those services.

The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete records stored only on your device. The app uses Android private app storage; no additional end-to-end encryption is claimed.

6. Support and policy changes

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve your request and retain correspondence as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. You can request deletion by contacting the same address, subject to those obligations. Please identify Hanteum Korean: Campus Life and avoid sending passwords, student IDs, or unnecessary personal information.

The pilot does not collect age profiles. It is an international-student learning tool and does not require an account. We update this policy and its effective date when the app’s processing changes. Features in other apps, including their advertising or recording tools, are not part of this pilot.
