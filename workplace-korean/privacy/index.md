# Privacy policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026.

App: **Workplace Korean: Learn Korean**, also referred to as Practical Workplace Korean or 한국일터. Android package: `com.minororbit.workplacekorean`. Developer/support contact: **[davidleepub@gmail.com](mailto:davidleepub@gmail.com)**. This policy applies only to this app.

## 1. Learning without an account

The app provides bundled Korean lessons, optional Hangul basics, quizzes, saved-expression review and activity badges. It does not require a user account or operate a developer learning-data server. The developer does not receive your local learning history through an analytics or crash-reporting backend. Advertising, Android speech services, backup services and websites you open can process information separately, as explained below.

## 2. Records on your device

The app stores lesson and saved-expression identifiers, learning positions, completion counts and dates, answers/progress used for practice, review records, learning/review days, language and voice preferences, audio speed, automatic playback, daily goals and helper preferences. It also stores local advertising-reward unlocks, ad-pause timing and helper greeting/rotation records.

The app-introduction badge records opening Android's share sheet, not whether a message was sent or whom it reached. These records are held in the app's private database or preferences and used for learning, resuming, settings and rewards. They are not uploaded to a developer learning server.

## 3. Optional microphone practice

The app asks for microphone permission when you choose to record speaking practice. You may decline and use other learning features. Recordings are kept in the app's private recordings folder so you can listen to yourself. Recording again for the same practice item replaces that item's previous audio.

The app does not upload recordings to the developer, advertising SDK or an AI service. It does not perform speech recognition or automated pronunciation scoring. Avoid recording others without their permission. This version has no dedicated recording-deletion control; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes recordings on that installation.

## 4. Android text-to-speech

The app passes lesson text to the installed Android text-to-speech engine to read it aloud. Voices and network requirements depend on the engine and selected voice. Some voices work offline; installing voice data, network voices and provider diagnostics may involve that provider's services and privacy policy. Practice recordings are not passed to the speech engine.

The installed app does not use a developer Google Cloud TTS, fal.ai or ElevenLabs API key to generate speech. Voice names or development samples are not an indication that those cloud APIs run inside the app.

## 5. Google advertising and privacy choices

This release uses Google Mobile Ads SDK **25.5.0** and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banner ads and optional rewarded ads involve Google and its advertising partners. The SDK can process IP addresses and inferred general location, device/account or advertising identifiers, interaction data, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and abuse prevention. The app does not request GPS location, contacts or your photo library.

Consent information is requested through UMP, and the app gates ad initialization using Google's can-request-ads result. A settings entry for advertising privacy choices is shown when UMP requires it. Available forms and choices depend on Google's configuration and region. Advertising-ID controls are also available in Android settings. A banner pause is not a promise that all SDK processing stops.

An earned optional reward pauses banner ads for two hours; a separate reward unlocks the additional lesson pack offered by the app. Rewards are stored locally, not paid purchases, cash or transferable account entitlements. Reward availability depends on the ad service and successful completion.

Google states that Mobile Ads SDK transmissions use TLS. Data processing and retention by Google and partners follow their policies, and may take place outside your country. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads), and [SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## 6. Android backup and device transfer

Android automatic backup is enabled. The app's backup rules include its database, shared preferences and settings DataStore. If enabled by your device/account configuration, the operating system may back up learning and settings data using its backup provider. The app excludes the recordings folder from its configured cloud backup, but includes recordings in configured device-to-device transfer. Actual backup/restore behavior depends on Android version, device and provider.

The app does not offer its own cloud synchronization or manual learning-backup export. Cloud backup copies are managed through your device/account's backup controls, not by the developer. Clearing local storage does not itself guarantee deletion of earlier operating-system backup copies, and restored data may reappear after reinstallation or device transfer.

## 7. Retention, deletion and security

Local data remains until it is removed through available item controls, replaced, cleared or the app is uninstalled. Unsaving an expression removes its saved-card association; it does not erase completed practice history. To delete local app data, use Android Settings → Apps → Workplace Korean → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall. This also removes local unlocks and settings, subject to later OS restore. Manage backup copies and installed speech data through their respective provider settings.

The developer cannot remotely inspect or erase data stored only on your device. Android private storage is used; this is not a promise of additional end-to-end encryption or protection on a compromised device. Advertising-service data retention/deletion is controlled by those services.

## 8. Links, sharing, support and audience

Optional Hangul resources, policy pages and voice-provider/download links open separate services. Those services may receive request/device information under their own policies. These documents use GitHub Pages hosting, which can process request information including IP addresses under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). GitHub Pages is not a learning-record backend.

The introduction-sharing feature gives preset app-introduction text to the app you choose in Android's share sheet. It does not attach your recordings or learning history. The selected app may transmit your message under its own policy.

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private recordings. The app is designed for adult workplace learners, not as a child-directed service. It does not collect an in-app age profile.

## 9. Contact and updates

For questions, privacy requests or errors, contact **[davidleepub@gmail.com](mailto:davidleepub@gmail.com)** and identify Workplace Korean. We update this policy's date when practices change and describe material changes through appropriate app or release information.
