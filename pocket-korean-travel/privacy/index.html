Privacy policy

HANTEUM: Travel Korean · Minor Orbit
Previous name: Pocket Korean: Korea Travel
com.minororbit.koreatravel
Effective date: 2026-11-02
Privacy contact: davidleepub@gmail.com

1. About this app

HANTEUM: Travel Korean (com.minororbit.koreatravel), by Minor Orbit, is for adult travelers. It has no account or developer-operated learning server. Lessons and discovery-map data are bundled. We do not add a separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service. Advertising SDKs, speech engines and external services have their own data processing.

2. Information stored on your device

Private app storage holds saved phrases, lesson and Hangul progress, quiz results, preferences, use dates and review-reminder choices, K-pop recommendation choices, ad consent, banner-pause times, local ad counters and verified ad settings. These learning records are not uploaded to us. The app cannot tell whether you have posted a store review. Local ad diagnostics contain result categories and numeric codes, not recordings or learning text; the app does not upload these logs.

3. Optional microphone recording

Microphone access is requested only for practice recording; declining does not block lessons. Recordings last up to 30 seconds. Save keeps the draft and replaces the saved recording for that phrase. You can delete recordings in the app. Audio is not uploaded to us, advertising services or an AI service; there is no speech recognition or automatic pronunciation scoring.

4. MP3 playback and Android speech

Bundled MP3s play locally without a speech-generation API call. Android text-to-speech can be selected or used if a recording is unavailable. Only fixed lesson text, not microphone recordings, is passed to the installed engine. Network use, voice downloads and diagnostics depend on that engine and provider. The app does not generate speech through Google Cloud TTS, fal.ai or ElevenLabs APIs during use.

5. Advertising and provider data

The app includes direct Unity Ads SDK 4.21.0 and Google AdMob SDK 25.5.0 with Google UMP. Defaults enable Unity and disable AdMob; verified remote settings can change enabled networks. If both are enabled and ready, initial requests select Unity 80% and AdMob 20%, with at most one fallback. This is not an impression guarantee. Unity is given contextual-only privacy flags; this does not mean no data is processed. Providers collect or share IP-derived approximate location, device/account or personal identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, functionality and fraud prevention. Unity also lists purchase history; this app has no in-app purchases and does not send learning records or recordings to either provider. Other app-activity collection depends on Unity's Acquire Optimization setting. Provider disclosures are linked below.

6. Advertising choices

Google UMP checks whether ads may be requested and shows a consent form where required. Settings shows ad privacy options when required. Android advertising-ID controls and Unity ad privacy controls offer additional choices, including provider-managed deletion requests. An optional rewarded video pauses banners for 2 hours after confirmed completion. Lessons remain available if you decline or skip. Pausing banners does not promise that all SDK processing stops.

7. Signed remote settings and GitHub

In the foreground the app checks a fixed HTTPS file on GitHub Pages at most once every 6 hours. GitHub receives connection data such as IP address and request metadata, not learning history or recordings. The app verifies the signature, app identity, version and permitted values. Settings only control ad-network enablement and initial weights, not code, arbitrary URLs, ad IDs or consent bypass. The last verified settings stay locally without expiry if offline or verification fails. This policy is also hosted on GitHub Pages.

8. Maps, learning websites and other links

The bundled map does not request GPS or provide live navigation. Opening Google Maps can send an attraction's name or coordinates, not your device location. Tourism, photo-source, speech-provider, HANTEUM Hangul/Korean and store links open external services, which may process connection data under their own policies. HANTEUM links include a supported language and course path, not your identity or learning records. Website progress is separate from app progress.

9. Sharing, reviews and exported files

Share app sends only the displayed promotional text and store link to the sharing app you choose; it does not attach recordings or learning history. Posting a review or opening the recommended K-pop app uses Google Play or your browser. A map-license ZIP exported through Android's document picker contains map data and attributions, not personal learning data. A chosen cloud document provider may upload that file.

10. Retention and deletion

Remove saved phrases and recordings with the app controls. Android Settings → Apps → HANTEUM: Travel Korean → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstalling, removes local progress, preferences, recordings, reminder choices and banner-pause records. Automatic Android backup and app-data transfer are disabled by the app's backup rules. Exported files and speech-engine downloads must be managed separately. We cannot remotely retrieve device-only data; providers manage retention and deletion of their own data.

11. Security, international processing and rights

Remote settings use HTTPS; Google and Unity state their SDK data is encrypted in transit. Private Android storage is used; additional end-to-end encryption is not claimed. Providers may process data outside your country under their policies. Privacy rights depend on applicable law; you may contact us about access, correction, deletion or objections, and contact the relevant provider for its data. UI language or distribution-country restrictions do not waive your rights.

12. Support, children and this website

Support email reveals your email address and what you send; we use it to answer and resolve requests and retain it as necessary for support and applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion, subject to those obligations. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private recordings. The app is not designed for children and does not collect age profiles. This policy site has no added analytics, advertising or forms; its entry page uses browser language locally to choose a translation, and you can switch languages. Changes are shown with a new effective date. Contact: davidleepub@gmail.com.
