Privacy policy

Pocket Korean: Korea Travel
Developer: Minor Orbit · Android package: com.minororbit.koreatravel
Effective date: October 23, 2026
Contact: davidleepub@gmail.com

1. What this app does

This independent app provides practical Korean lessons, Hangul learning, saved-phrase review, a Korea sights map and travel tips. It is intended for adult travelers. No account, login or developer-operated learning server is used. Learning content and discovery-map data are bundled with the app. We do not include a separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service. Advertising, speech engines and external websites can process data as described below.

2. Learning information on your device

Saved expression identifiers, lesson and Hangul progress, recent learning positions, review results and display or speech preferences are kept in private app storage. Advertising consent and the timing of a temporary banner pause are also stored locally. We do not upload your bookmarks, quiz answers or learning history to a developer server.

3. Optional microphone recordings

Microphone permission is requested only when you choose to record practice. You may refuse permission and use other features. Recordings last up to 30 seconds. A draft is kept only if you choose Save; saving a new recording replaces the previous recording for that expression. You can play and delete saved recordings in the app. The app does not upload them to the developer, advertising services or an AI service and does not perform speech recognition or automated pronunciation scoring. Avoid recording other people or sensitive personal information.

4. Android speech

Korean listening can use bundled fixed MP3 recordings, played locally without a generation API request. You can alternatively select your installed Android text-to-speech engine. The app supplies fixed learning text to that engine, not your microphone recordings. Speech processing, downloaded voices, network use and diagnostics depend on the engine and voice you select and its provider's policy. Installing speech software or voices can require internet access. The app does not contain user-triggered Google Cloud TTS, fal.ai or ElevenLabs API generation.

5. Advertising: Google AdMob and Unity Ads

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0) and Google's User Messaging Platform. Banner ads and an optional rewarded video are provided. Google states that its SDK automatically collects and shares IP addresses, app/ad interactions, diagnostic information and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location even though this app does not request GPS permissions or show your current location.

Advertising processing can involve Google and advertising partners outside your country. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Consent information is requested and a form is shown where required by Google's service. When required, an Ad privacy options control appears in Settings. You can also use Android advertising-ID controls. A rewarded video pauses banners for two hours after the advertising provider confirms the reward. It does not lock or unlock lessons, and pausing banners is not a promise that all SDK processing stops.

See Google privacy policy, Google advertising information and Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

The app also uses the direct Unity Ads SDK (4.21.0), independently of LevelPlay. Requests normally start with AdMob (80%) or Unity Ads (20%); a failed initialization or load can fall back once to the other available provider. These probabilities do not guarantee an impression ratio. Unity receives contextual-only privacy flags; this does not mean no data is processed.

Unity's SDK disclosure lists collection and sharing of approximate location, personal identifiers, purchase history, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers for purposes including advertising, analytics, functionality and fraud prevention. Collection of other app activity depends on the Acquire Optimization setting. This app has no in-app purchases and does not send your practice recordings, saved expressions, quiz answers or lesson history to Unity. Unity states SDK data is encrypted in transit. For provider-managed privacy and deletion choices, use the privacy control in the Unity ad and see Unity privacy policy and Unity Ads data disclosure.

6. Maps, websites and files

The bundled MapLibre discovery map does not request GPS location or provide live navigation. Opening Google Maps, public tourism information, a photo source, a speech-provider page or this policy contacts the chosen external service. It may receive normal web-request information under its own policy. Choosing a place can pass that place's name or coordinates to the map service; these are the attraction's location, not your device location.

The map-license export uses Android's document picker to save a ZIP containing bundled map data and attribution files. It does not contain recordings or learning history. A document provider you choose may upload the exported file; delete that file separately if needed.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting can process request information such as IP addresses under GitHub's privacy statement; it is not a learning-data backend.

7. Retention, deletion and security

Local data stays until removed through available app controls, Android Clear storage, or uninstalling. Remove bookmarks or recordings individually in the app. To remove all local records, use Android Settings → Apps → Pocket Korea Travel → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall. This removes progress, preferences and banner-pause records too. The app disables automatic Android backup and excludes its data from cloud backup and device transfer. Exported files and speech-engine downloads are managed separately.

We cannot remotely retrieve or delete data held only on your device. Google and other providers control retention and deletion of the data they process. Private Android app storage is used; no additional end-to-end encryption is claimed.

8. Support and changes

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you send and use them to respond and resolve the request. Do not send passwords, private recordings or unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the issue and applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion, subject to those obligations. The app does not collect age profiles and is not designed for children.

Contact davidleepub@gmail.com for privacy questions. We update this page and its effective date when practices change.
